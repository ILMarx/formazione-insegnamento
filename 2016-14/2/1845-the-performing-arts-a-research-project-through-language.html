Title: The performing arts. A research project through language, emotions and movement

Abstract: Learning foreign languages through the performing arts takes on alwaysmore interest in the International scientific world. The essay presents anempirical research conducted in a secondary school of Rome, by the use ofa specific methodology, the drama pedagogy.
