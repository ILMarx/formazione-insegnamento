Title: The Contribution of Scholastic Institution and Sport Against the Youth Doping

Abstract: The purpose of this research work is to highlight the contribution that sportcan provide for a sustainable educational and training development foryoung athletes.There are, of course, some basic social and cultural criticalities to be overcomein order to assert the values of sport; so it is necessary to counter thegrowing phenomenon of youth doping.
