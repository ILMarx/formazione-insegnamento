Title: Between emotions and knowledge.From the conflict to integration into the horizons of empathy

Abstract: The work aims to outline an evolutionary parable of research on the relationship emotions and knowledge, which highlights, for the past, the elements of profound connection between the contributions of philosophy, neuroscientific and psychological and psychoanalytic research;For the present, his trespassing towards disciplinary, study and research areas that are traditionally foreign to it and which give it value, as well as within the care relationships, also in terms of the historical social experience.
