Abstract: The article deals with the studies and research on teachers' beliefs.'80s' 80s examined several aspects of the teachers' thought processes (Calderhead, 1996; Woolfolk, Hoy, 2006).The beliefs of the players have been considered to understand how the teachers conceptualize their work, make decisions and choose the practices. A growing part of the research claims that beliefs should be examined context, considering cultural influences (Olson, 1988; Mansour, 2008).Beyond the social factors that influence beliefs, many gallows ask if the beliefs can change and have been developed on conceptual change (Mason, 2003). Une partie croissante de la recherche affirme que les croyances devraient être examinées le contexte, compte tenu des influences culturelles (Olson, 1988; Mansour, 2008).Au-delà des facteurs sociaux qui influencent les croyances, de nombreuses pochettes demandent si les croyances peuvent changer et ont été développées sur le changement conceptuel (Mason, 2003).
