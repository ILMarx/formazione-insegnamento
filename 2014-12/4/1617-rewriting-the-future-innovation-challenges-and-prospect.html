Title: Rewriting the Future: innovation, challenges and prospects of educational research and training

Abstract: The paper examine the social contexts of innovation related to educationalproblems typical of our time. The goal is to center the focus on the possibilitiesof educational research as absolute resource of innovation to face thechallenges of change and to support adults, especially parents, to growthpaths that involve and characterize families.
