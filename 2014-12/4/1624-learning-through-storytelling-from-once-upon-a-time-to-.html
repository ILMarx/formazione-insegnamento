Title: Learning through storytelling: from Once upon a time to Digital Storytelling

Abstract: The storytelling is an interesting process as it is both a tool in the cognitivere-elaboration of content and values and an instrument for socially transmitting,sharing and re-writing knowledge. Therefore, the purpose of thisessay is to demonstrate the effectiveness of Digital Storytelling as a learningactivity.
