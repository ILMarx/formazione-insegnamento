Title: Diversity education in the scholastic context

Abstract: The purpose of the paper is to propose the model of Diversity Managementas a tool capable to promoting greater diversity education in the schoolcontext.This management approach allows you to obtain, at the level of the enterprise,considerable benefits and it is assumed that, through the applicationof its principles, can stimulate the acceptance and development of the subjectsperceived "different" for racial issues, physical, religious, ethnic, etc,also in the school system.If this were to happen, thanks to a work done from the origin of this socialcriticality, you might develop a huge cultural change aimed at integration ofdiversity, with a consequent social benefit and a future economic benefit.
